The path

Keep the island voice. Add the partner only where it belongs.

  1. Fit review

    Category, season, and whether the brand can sit beside official roads, weather, and regional guides without looking rented.

  2. Unit map

    Hero, in-feed, and region placements. Reykjavík, South, North, Reykjanes first. West, East, and Westfjords when the story needs them.

  3. Creative + tracking

    Partner creative in IcelandNOW tokens. EvolveX360 measurement on the click path — no orphan landing.

  4. Live ops

    Inbox, reputation, and reporting stay on the EvolveX360 stack. The partner keeps the brand; we keep the system.
