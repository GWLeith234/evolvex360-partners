01 · Arrive

They land. The stay begins.

Wheels down in Iceland. The family has a rental and a room. The trip is open. They still need a guide for what to do today.

02 · Meet Sylvia

Hi, I'm Sylvia, your AI travel expert.

I know Iceland the way a local guide knows it on a good day: what is open, what is worth the drive, and what matches the guests in front of you. Taste, timing, weather windows, and the trips that fit this stay, not a generic brochure list.

That intelligence comes from swarms of researchers and reporters who keep IcelandNOW updating. News, events, attractions, and on-the-ground signals land in the feed Sylvia reads. She stays current because the feed stays current.

Guests meet me where the trip already happens: the hotel lobby, the rental car, the airport, or a scan of your QR. Same guide every time, tagged to you as the Partner who opened the door. That makes the trip easier. They ask once, get a clear next step, and keep moving.

03 · Always on

Sylvia stays with them for the whole trip

Guests can talk to Sylvia anytime during the stay. Behind her, AI agents are scouring data and social chatter so advice tracks what is happening now. That includes weather alerts and impassable roads, not only attraction highlights.

IcelandNOW is the foundation Sylvia sits on. The live feed is the source. Sylvia is the guide on top of it.

She stays polite and de-escalates when a guest is frustrated or stuck. We monitor conversations and learn from them so the next guest gets a cleaner answer, and so Partners get help that protects the stay, not noise that burns goodwill.
